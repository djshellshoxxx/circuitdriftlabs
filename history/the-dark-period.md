# The Dark Period

**Fictional CDL lore / not company history**

The years the archive refuses to simplify.

<section><h2>The missing shelf</h2><p>In this fictional chapter, an archive move scatters handwritten schematics and unlabelled tapes. Later engineers inherit conclusions without experimental conditions. The loss becomes an organizing metaphor for everything a modern lab ought to preserve.</p></section><section><h2>The reconstruction rule</h2><p>No reconstruction may quietly become an original. A recovered diagram carries its uncertainty; an inferred component value stays marked as inferred. The Bureau of Spectral Preservation emerges in the story to keep the provenance beside the result.</p></section><section><h2>The lesson at the bench</h2><p>The story informs a practical editorial rule: distinguish measurements, models and recollections. An evocative account is not evidence of a deployment, discovery or institutional affiliation.</p></section>

The Dark Period

The years the archive refuses to simplify.

The missing shelf

In this fictional chapter, an archive move scatters handwritten schematics and unlabelled tapes. Later engineers inherit conclusions without experimental conditions. The loss becomes an organizing metaphor for everything a modern lab ought to preserve.

The reconstruction rule

No reconstruction may quietly become an original. A recovered diagram carries its uncertainty; an inferred component value stays marked as inferred. The Bureau of Spectral Preservation emerges in the story to keep the provenance beside the result.

The lesson at the bench

The story informs a practical editorial rule: distinguish measurements, models and recollections. An evocative account is not evidence of a deployment, discovery or institutional affiliation.

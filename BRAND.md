# Brand notes

## Working name: Circuit Drift Lab

“Circuit” points to the tools; “Drift” leaves room for effects, instruments and utilities that move and change. “Lab” describes a place to test sounds without making claims about a large company. Short form: **CDL**. Mark: a single waveform crossing a square circuit frame.

Two other names that would fit the same design: **Stray Signal Lab** (more experimental) and **Phase Current Lab** (more engineering focused). These are creative suggestions, not trademark clearances.

## Visual system

Near-black `#0E1116`, charcoal `#171B22`, orange `#E8532A`, teal `#4FB6C4`. A restrained oscilloscope and signal-routing vocabulary ties the site to the VST interface without pretending to be a plugin. The drawings are reproducible SVG paths and schematic shapes, not generated imagery.

## Company description

Circuit Drift Lab designs audio tools for people who make music by listening closely and changing one thing at a time. We specialize in open source technologies for the major DAWs, building instruments, effects and utilities around clear signal flow and hands-on control. Some tools are subtle. Others pull a sound apart. Every one should make it easy to find something of your own.

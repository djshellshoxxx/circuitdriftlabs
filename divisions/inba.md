# Institute for Nonlinear Behavioral Acoustics

**Engineering prospectus**

Where a transfer curve becomes a listening question.

<section><h2>Research remit</h2><p>INBA is the proposed theoretical and perceptual branch of CDL, connecting distortion models with the Cognitive & Psychoacoustics Group. Its agenda includes spatial localization, listener fatigue and the perception of time-varying nonlinearities.</p></section><section><h2>Evidence at the bench</h2><p>Use level-matched, randomized listening comparisons.

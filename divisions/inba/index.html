Institute for Nonlinear Behavioral Acoustics

Where a transfer curve becomes a listening question.

Research remit

INBA is the proposed theoretical and perceptual branch of CDL, connecting distortion models with the Cognitive & Psychoacoustics Group. Its agenda includes spatial localization, listener fatigue and the perception of time-varying nonlinearities.

Evidence at the bench

Use level-matched, randomized listening comparisons. Separate preference from discrimination, report uncertainty, and retain anonymized trial design and analysis scripts.

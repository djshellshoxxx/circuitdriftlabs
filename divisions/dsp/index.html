DSP Research Division

Circuit models. Bounded computation. Audible evidence.

Research remit

Volterra models, wave digital filters and nonlinear state-space methods form a proposed research track for studying analog behavior. The central question is how closely a model can follow a reference circuit within a predictable audio-thread budget.

Evidence at the bench

Record residual error, iteration count, alias energy and worst-case callback time. Compare against a high-rate offline reference; preserve the input signals and parameter trajectories.

# The CDL lexicon

**Fictional CDL lore / not company history**

A small vocabulary for a very particular workbench.

<dl><dt>The Ledger</dt><dd>The fictional leather-bound notebook at the heart of CDL lore. In practical work, its counterpart is a version-controlled experiment record.</dd><dt>Project G-74</dt><dd>A narrative codename for the imagined discrete-circuit lineage.</dd><dt>The Challis Modification</dt><dd>A fictional bench designation; not a released product or verified historical invention.</dd><dt>The 1982 Bench Draft</dt><dd>An archival-style label used in the fictional setting, not a software version.</dd><dt>Sheffield Thermal Drift Index</dt><dd>An invented term in the lore. Technical documents must use defined, measurable quantities instead.</dd><dt>Bureau of Spectral Preservation</dt><dd>The proposed archival discipline: preserve signals, conditions and provenance together.</dd></dl>

Traditions of the workbench

Question the model. Keep the evidence.

The Zero-Crossing Conclave

An imagined annual review in which engineers defend a signal topology with equations, reproducible traces and listening examples. The useful tradition is rigorous criticism of the work, with room to revise a conclusion.

The Ledger ritual

In the story, every algorithm begins as a handwritten derivation. The practical counterpart is a short design note that explains assumptions, units, boundary conditions and failure behavior before implementation.

The null test

The proposed closing ritual is a comparison against a reference. A remaining difference is investigated rather than celebrated automatically. The aim is understanding, including when the hypothesis fails.

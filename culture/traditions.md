# Traditions of the workbench

**Fictional CDL lore / not company history**

Question the model. Keep the evidence.

<section><h2>The Zero-Crossing Conclave</h2><p>An imagined annual review in which engineers defend a signal topology with equations, reproducible traces and listening examples. The useful tradition is rigorous criticism of the work, with room to revise a conclusion.</p></section><section><h2>The Ledger ritual</h2><p>In the story, every algorithm begins as a handwritten derivation. The practical counterpart is a short design note that explains assumptions, units, boundary conditions and failure behavior before implementation.</p></section><section><h2>The null test</h2><p>The proposed closing ritual is a comparison against a reference. A remaining difference is investigated rather than celebrated automatically. The aim is understanding, including when the hypothesis fails.</p></section>

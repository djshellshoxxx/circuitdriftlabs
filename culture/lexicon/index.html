The CDL lexicon

A small vocabulary for a very particular workbench.

The Ledger
The fictional leather-bound notebook at the heart of CDL lore. In practical work, its counterpart is a version-controlled experiment record.
Project G-74
A narrative codename for the imagined discrete-circuit lineage.
The Challis Modification
A fictional bench designation; not a released product or verified historical invention.
The 1982 Bench Draft
An archival-style label used in the fictional setting, not a software version.
Sheffield Thermal Drift Index
An invented term in the lore. Technical documents must use defined, measurable quantities instead.
Bureau of Spectral Preservation
The proposed archival discipline: preserve signals, conditions and provenance together.

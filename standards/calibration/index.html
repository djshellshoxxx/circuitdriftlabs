Proposed bench protocol

Calibration & measurement protocol

Every trace needs a reference.

Before capture

Record device identification, signal chain, gain settings, sample rate, clock source and environment. Verify reference level and loopback behavior before connecting the device under test. Use documented warm-up and settling intervals.

During capture

Capture silence, stepped levels, sweeps and relevant transients without changing undocumented settings. Keep raw files immutable. Repeat the sequence to distinguish drift from measurement variability.

After capture

Report alignment, windowing, normalization and excluded samples. Keep measurement uncertainty separate from model residuals. Store raw captures, analysis scripts and a human-readable summary together.

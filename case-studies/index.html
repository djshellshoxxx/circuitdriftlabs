Application briefs / prospective

Industry application studies

Four demanding contexts for audio engineering.

Broadcast, film & immersive media

Proposed evaluation: dialogue processing and spatial rendering in a post-production chain. Compare artifact audibility, channel alignment and loudness behavior. Dolby Atmos is a workflow reference, not a claimed partnership or certification.

Automotive acoustic engineering

Proposed evaluation: cabin acoustics, active noise control and EV sound design. A study would need measured transfer paths, operating conditions and a controlled simulator before any deployment claim.

Interactive gaming & procedural audio

Proposed evaluation: event-driven procedural sound under variable voice counts. Wwise and FMOD are potential integration targets; no shipping CDL middleware integration or AAA deployment is asserted.

Biomedical & therapeutic audio

Exploratory questions include auditory-model simulation and soundscape perception. These are research directions, not clinical products, validated therapies or claims of treatment efficacy.

Deployment register

No customer deployments or post-mortems are documented in this collection. Future case studies should identify the project, permission to publish, method, baseline, measured outcome and limitations.

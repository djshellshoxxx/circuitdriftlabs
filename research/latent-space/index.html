Study design / no empirical results

Perceptual evaluation of latent-space interpolation

A smooth coordinate path does not guarantee a smooth sound.

Experimental question

Does equal movement through a latent representation produce approximately equal perceived timbral change? Compare linear paths with a perceptually reparameterized path using the same endpoint sounds and fixed loudness conditions.

Study protocol

Generate fixed-duration stimuli at evenly spaced path positions. Randomize ordering and ask listeners to compare adjacent perceptual distances. Keep training examples separate from evaluation material, and document synthesis state, random seeds and level matching.

Analysis and limits

Inspect spectral change, transient continuity and listener ratings together. Report sample size, uncertainty and the distribution of individual responses. A spectral-distance improvement is not, by itself, evidence of improved listener preference. No listener study results are claimed here.

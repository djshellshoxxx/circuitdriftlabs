04 / THE LAB04

A small lab for sounds and workflows that deserve better tools.

Circuit Drift Labs designs open-source audio tools for people who make music by listening closely, experimenting and solving practical problems. The work spans instruments, effects, diagnostics and browser utilities for the major DAWs and surrounding studio workflow.

Some tools will be subtle. Others will pull a sound apart. The common thread is a direct, useful interface that makes it easy to get somewhere interesting.
